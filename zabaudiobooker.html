ZabAudioBooker

Turn a Markdown file into an audiobook. Kokoro-82M runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

You opened this page straight from your filesystem. That works in Chrome and Edge, model caching included. If your browser blocks the model download from here, serve the folder instead: npx serve . or python3 -m http.server 8000, then open the address it prints, e.g. http://localhost:8000/zabaudiobooker.html.
